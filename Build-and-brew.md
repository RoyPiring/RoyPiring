## 🌱 What this is

Build & Brew is the NextWork live cohort, and I run it as the Build Master. Each week the room builds one real, end-to-end solutions engineering project together on the NextWork Discord. You can build along or just watch. Every build lands in a GitHub repo, finished or not. The point is plain: practice, and proof you can do the work. No job promises, and no resume help. Just the reps, in the open.

## 🎯 What each build drills

Every build takes one point of view, the high-performing solutions engineer, across twelve roles from Foundation to Advanced. Three things are drilled until they are reflex:

- **Design before you build.** The decision records and the diagram come before the code.
- **Prove it or it did not happen.** Every build ends in a concrete check, never "it looks right."
- **Do not trust the confident answer.** Every build scores the AI and reports where it was wrong.

## 🗂️ The repo

Every build is documented as a system, with its architecture diagram, an implementation map, and the check that proved it works, the same way as my engineering repos.
